# Website home

Status: working proposal for review; visual direction awaits the user's feedback.
Mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A working review sheet: contextual feedback is the first proof visitors
can try. Keep the example, its selected target, and its conversation together.

**OWN-WORLD:** The approved Focus mark, yellow (#e2f276), and ink (#292c31).
Onest page typography and the DM Sans wordmark. Flat light surfaces, thin meaningful
rules, compact corners, numbered pins, and plainly named controls.

**STORY:** Understand visual reviews for Vite, select an example target, leave and
resolve a note, then reach the integration instructions. All sample content is
labeled; the pending npm release is stated honestly.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact navigation row; a two-line offer beside a short
explanation and primary action; the live review example immediately below. The
selected heading and conversation are visible together on desktop. Phones stack
the sample and conversation; selecting a target moves to the focused note form.

**FORM:** Code-first working proposal, interactive review sheet. The user confirmed
a working page and disposable browser-local demo without accounts/shared reviews.
The exploratory seed was recorded after the initial working page, not an approved
concept choice: seed:5155ade11456a2c0. No approved comp or quality-bar card exists.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Boundaries

Keep real plugin regression coverage in separate fixtures. The public build must
not mount the development widget or expose its API. Demo state exists in memory
and disappears on reload. This is a functional proposal, not a production deployment.
